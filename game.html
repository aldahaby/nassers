Health & Safety

Photosensitive seizure warning

A very small number of people may have a seizure when exposed to flashing lights or patterns, including while playing video games — even with no history of seizures or epilepsy. Symptoms can include dizziness, altered vision, eye or face twitching, jerking of limbs, disorientation, confusion, or loss of awareness. Stop playing immediately and consult a doctor if you experience any of these. Parents should watch for these signs in children.

This Game contains flashing lights, strobing neon, rapid camera motion and screen shake.

Reduce the risk

Play in a well-lit room, sit further from the screen, use the smallest comfortable screen, lower your device brightness, and stop as soon as you feel unwell.

Motion sensitivity

Fast forward motion can cause nausea, dizziness or disorientation in some players. If that happens, stop playing and rest until the symptoms pass.

Repetitive strain and fatigue

Take a 10–15 minute break every hour. Stop if your hands, wrists, arms or eyes become tired or sore, and see a doctor if discomfort persists.

Play with awareness

Do not play while walking, cycling, driving or anywhere you need your attention for your own safety. Sound is on by default — keep the volume low enough to hear what is around you.
