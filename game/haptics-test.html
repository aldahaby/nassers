2 · Vibration API

Works on Android/Chrome. On iPhone this button does nothing at all — Safari has never shipped this API.

3 · iOS switch tap — your finger

Flip this switch with your finger. This is the only haptic a web page can ask for on iOS. If you feel a tick here, your phone and iOS version can do it.

Toggle me

4 · iOS switch tap — from code

The same switch, flipped by a script instead of your finger. This is what the game has to do for a smash or a crash. If 3 buzzed and this one doesn't, that is the whole problem.

5 · Inside your tap

A code-driven flip fired during your finger press, while the activation is still live. This is the trick the game uses for the FIRE and SURGE buttons.

6 · Native app bridge

Only present when the game is running inside an app wrapper, never in a browser.

What to tell me

Just the numbers that buzzed — e.g. “3 yes, 4 no, 5 yes”. That tells me exactly how much of the game's haptics can work on your iPhone without an app build.
